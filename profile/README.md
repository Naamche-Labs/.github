Most AI comes from Western labs where English is the default. We build for the **32 million people who speak Nepali** — open models, public benchmarks, and reproducible code, released from Kathmandu, Nepal.

### 🎙️ Kriti — open Nepali speech recognition

Our first model: an open **119M-parameter Nepali speech-to-text (ASR)** model. It shares **rank #1 of 19** evaluated systems at **24.08% word error rate**, with the lowest raw WER in the field. Open weights, MIT.

&nbsp;&nbsp;→ [Model page](https://naamchelabs.com/models/kriti-v1) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/harrrshall/kriti) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [Code](https://github.com/Naamche-Labs/kriti)
